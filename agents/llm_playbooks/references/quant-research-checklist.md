# Quant Research Checklist

1. Is data fresh and quality-passed?
2. Is the market regime supportive?
3. Are trend and momentum aligned?
4. Is liquidity enough for the proposed size?
5. Is volatility/drawdown acceptable?
6. Does relative strength support selection?
7. Does QAOA agree with TA ranking?
8. Does the portfolio obey constraints?
9. Is the rationale grounded in supplied data?
10. Would this decision still make sense after fees/taxes?
