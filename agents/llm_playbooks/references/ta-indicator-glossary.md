# TA Indicator Glossary

- `RSI 14`: momentum oscillator. Neutral zone is often 40-60; strong trends can remain high or low.
- `MACD histogram`: difference between MACD and signal line; positive supports momentum.
- `ADX 14`: trend strength; above 20 suggests stronger trend.
- `ATR pct`: ATR divided by close; higher means wider execution/risk buffer.
- `Bollinger band width`: volatility expansion/contraction proxy.
- `MFI 14`: volume-weighted momentum oscillator.
- `Relative strength`: symbol return minus benchmark return.
- `Beta vs VN30`: systematic sensitivity to VN30 returns.
